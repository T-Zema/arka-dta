DTA StarCraft Mod + ARKA campaign - changelog
==============================================

A mod for Dawn of the Tiberium Age: StarCraft factions, units and buildings, new engine patches
and the mashup campaign "ARKA: Sign Error" (Red Alert x StarCraft). For private use: the StarCraft
graphics, sounds and music come from a legal copy of StarCraft: Remastered and belong to Blizzard
Entertainment.

DTA StarCraft Mod + kampania ARKA - lista zmian
================================================

Mod do Dawn of the Tiberium Age: frakcje, jednostki i budynki ze StarCrafta, nowe łatki silnika
i kampania mashup "ARKA: Błąd znaku" (Red Alert x StarCraft). Do prywatnego użytku: grafika,
dźwięki i muzyka StarCrafta pochodzą z legalnej kopii StarCraft: Remastered i należą do Blizzard
Entertainment.


Version 2026.10.30 - Terran and Protoss computer players, ARKA #3, the campaign in alpha (for DTA 16.2.3)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

INSTALLATION

- Run the new DTA-SCMod-Setup.exe and click "Update StarCraft Mod". The previous version does not
  have to be uninstalled.
- When the installation is done, the installer says where to find the campaign: the ARKA campaign
  (alpha, missions #0-#5) is playable under New Mission.

WHAT THE MOD CONTAINS SO FAR

- Three StarCraft factions in skirmish, next to the DTA ones:
    - Terran: 18 StarCraft buildings, a Construction Tank that deploys into a Command Center,
      12 units (the starships fly as vehicles), 11 research upgrades as building upgrades.
    - Zerg: Drones morph into buildings, creep grows from Hatcheries, Creep Tumors and colonies,
      Zerg buildings stand only on creep, every Overlord adds storage for 100 credits (like a
      silo), 13 research upgrades, campaign heroes, the Zerg EVA and sidebar.
    - Protoss: buildings need the power field of a Pylon, Probe and Nexus as in StarCraft,
      research upgrades.
- The STARCRAFT checkbox: StarCraft units and buildings for every faction, Zerg units from any
  Barracks; Marines and Firebats as real infantry with the Stim Pack (an engine patch lets
  infantry "deploy").
- StarCraft workers (SCV, Drone, Probe) with mining animations, a Refinery takes 2 SCVs.
- StarCraft sounds when you select a building, the 23 StarCraft music tracks.
- The StarCraft maps (4)Lost Temple and (4)Fighting Spirit.
- The campaign "ARKA: Sign Error", alpha: missions #0-#5 can be played from start to victory,
  with 3 difficulty levels (EASY, MEDIUM, HARD).
- The installer and uninstaller (English and Polish) that puts the original DTA files back.

TERRAN COMPUTER PLAYER (new)

Before this version the computer playing Terran built Allied copies, had no attack plan and never
made an army. Now it plays with StarCraft units only. It needs the STARCRAFT and Advanced AI
checkboxes (Advanced AI is on by default).

- Only StarCraft: Terran no longer owns 115 non-StarCraft types (Allied buildings, the DTA
  harvester and MCV, walls, Transport, helicopters...). This also holds for a human Terran.
- Base: the computer deploys the Construction Tank, builds Supply Depots (later Uber Supply
  Depots) as power, a Refinery with SCVs (it no longer sells the Refinery as "without harvesters"),
  Barracks, Factory, Bunkers, Missile Turrets and a Starport. Then one of each other building
  (Academy, Armory, Comsat, Science Facility, Nuclear Silo...) and all 12 research upgrades, like
  the StarCraft AI.
- 12 ground plans, the first one that fits is used:
    - early game: an infantry rush, a scout, home defence while it has no Factory;
    - bio push: Marines and Firebats after the Academy (as in StarCraft melee);
    - vehicle rush once the Factory stands;
    - Vulture raids on your harvesters (when you have more than one refinery);
    - a large raid when you have many refineries, a flank attack on badly guarded refineries;
    - hold the line: a home guard when you are twice as strong;
    - siege push: Siege Tanks with infantry (as the StarCraft AI of "The Trump Card");
    - direct and fast attacks as a fallback. The attacks grow over time.
- Air fleet: Wraiths, Battlecruisers and Science Vessels from the Starport attack as a fleet
  (harass and late fleet attacks, like the Battlecruisers of the StarCraft AI).
- Siege Mode (engine patch): the computer sieges a tank only when a target is in range (11.9
  cells as a tank, 20.4 sieged, at least 3) and turns it back into a tank after about 2250 frames
  without a target. Before, it sieged at once wherever the tank stood and never packed up. The DTA
  Allies AI also deploys its Enforcer only near the enemy now.
- Self-defence: 34 Terran combat units react within 12 cells (more for weapons with a longer
  range). When you attack a building of the computer, its free units and the squad it is
  gathering come and fight. An attack on a Bunker or turret alone does not call for help. Your own
  units are affected only in Guard Area mode.
- Difficulty: the same 7 AI levels as the DTA factions.
- It will not build Medics, Dropships or Valkyries to fight, makes no expansions on StarCraft
  maps (the AI looks for Tiberium trees) and does not attack without Advanced AI.

PROTOSS COMPUTER PLAYER (new)

Same conditions: STARCRAFT and Advanced AI.

- Only StarCraft: the DTA harvester no longer belongs to Protoss.
- Base: the computer unpacks its Nexus, builds Pylons as power (more Pylons when the power gets
  low, like supply in StarCraft), an Assimilator, Gateway and Robotics Facility, then one of each
  of 15 buildings (Forge, Photon Cannon, Cybernetics Core, Shield Battery, Stargate, Citadel of
  Adun, Robotics Support Bay, Observatory, Templar Archives, Fleet Beacon, Arbiter Tribunal...)
  and 17 research upgrades. Photon Cannons are its base and air defence.
- 10 ground plans: an early Zealot rush, a scout, early defence (2 Zealots and a Dragoon),
  a wave of 12 Zealots (attack 1 of the StarCraft melee AI), Dark Templar raids on your
  harvesters (only when you have no detectors), a flank attack on refineries, hold the line,
  the Gateway army (Zealots, Dragoons, Templars, growing over time), direct and fast attacks.
- 3 plans for the Robotics Facility and Stargate units: a Reaver and Archon push, Scout harass,
  and a fleet of Scouts and Carriers that grows over time.
- Engine patch: Probes and a new Nexus come from the Robotics Facility, but the engine put them in
  the ground factory queue, where nothing could take them; after the first lost Probe the computer
  would have stopped building. Now they go to the right queue, and the Robotics Facility and
  Stargate are placed on land.
- It will not build Corsairs, Shuttles or Observers. Without Advanced AI it only builds and
  defends.
- For the campaign (#6): a script module for a Protoss base in a mission, with the attack waves
  of the StarCraft campaign (first wave after about 3 minutes, then every 2 minutes; Dark Templar
  raids on harvesters; destroying every Pylon stops the waves).

"ARKA: SIGN ERROR" CAMPAIGN (alpha)

- New mission #3 Signal from the Sky (Sygnał z nieba), Antiga Prime 2499: a covert mission without
  an MCV, on a map made from the StarCraft mission "Revolution" with your base from #4 in the
  corner. The cloaked Kerrigan has to switch off 2 observation towers (get within 3 cells),
  free a prison camp (6 rebels join you), and when the Zerg arrive you choose: destroy Mengsk's
  psi emitter or leave it. Victory: destroy the Command Center of the Confederate garrison. If
  Kerrigan is hit or a tower is fired at, the alarm goes off: a chase at the tower and a squad
  against your base every 7 minutes. Confederate patrols raid your refinery; 4 money crates.
  Difficulty levels and objective reminders as in the other missions.
- The campaign list now has missions #0, #1, #2, #3, #4 and #5. #6 Fire from the Sky and
  #7 Betrayal are being built (maps only, not in the list yet).

BALANCE

- Melee units react to enemies 1 cell farther (not their sight, not their attack range), in
  skirmish and in all ARKA missions:
    - Zergling 2.75 -> 3.75, Devouring One 3.28 -> 4.28, Zealot 3.75 -> 4.75;
    - new: Dark Templar 2.75, Ultralisk and Torrasque 3.5, Broodling 2.93, Infested
      Kerrigan 3.1.
  Drones, Infested Terrans, Scourges and Firebats are left as they were.

OTHER WORK

- New neutral StarCraft buildings for maps: Power Generator, Starbase, Psi Disrupter, Ion Cannon
  and Stasis Cell (used in #3: a space station and a city generator in the suburbs, an ion cannon
  in the garrison).
- The StarCraft map converter makes better coasts: no more paved crosses or squares of earth in
  lakes, bays are no longer taken for bridges, narrow straits are widened, the ends of lakes are
  closed, tiny ponds are filled.
- #6 Fire from the Sky (Ogień z nieba): the terrain from the unreleased StarCraft map "Biting the
  Bullet" (Antiga), 4 causeways across the channels, the colony Antioch on the south-east island
  built from StarCraft Terran buildings. No triggers yet.
- #7 Betrayal (Zdrada): the terrain from the StarCraft mission "The Trump Card", a Dominion city of
  6 districts, a port with the Hyperion, Duke with tanks. No triggers yet.
- Studied: the StarCraft AI scripts (all 293, used as models for the plans above), the DTA
  Advanced AI inside Vinifera.dll, how the AI difficulty levels work.
- Installer: the line about the campaign after the installation.

TEST STATUS

- Tested in the game: the Terran computer player on Easy AI (it builds, makes an army and
  attacks).
- Not tested yet: the Protoss computer player, the Siege Mode patch, the Terran self-defence,
  mission #3, the neutral buildings, the melee reaction ranges. The maps passed the generator
  checks; the installer passed the 5 sandbox tests (install, re-install, uninstall, install over
  files already in place, repair after a DTA update).

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

- The campaign is an alpha: it can have bugs, and its briefings and dialogue are in Polish only.
- The computer cannot play Zerg (it does not place its Drones as buildings). Do not give Zerg to
  a computer player.
- Terran and Protoss computer players need Advanced AI and make no expansions on StarCraft maps.
- The music files lie loose in the MUSIC folder; that the game finds them there is not checked yet.

KOMPUTER GRA TERRANAMI (nowe)

Przed tą wersją komputer grający Terranami budował kopie budynków Aliantów, nie miał planu ataku
i nigdy nie robił armii. Teraz gra wyłącznie jednostkami StarCrafta. Potrzebuje checkboxów
STARCRAFT i Advanced AI (Advanced AI jest domyślnie włączone).

- Tylko StarCraft: Terran nie ma już 115 typów spoza StarCrafta (budynki Aliantów, zbieracz i MCV
  DTA, mury, Transport, śmigłowce...). Dotyczy to też gracza-człowieka grającego Terranami.
- Baza: komputer rozkłada Construction Tank, buduje Supply Depoty (później Uber Supply Depoty)
  jako zasilanie, Refinery z SCV (nie sprzedaje już Refinery jako "bez zbieraczy"), Barracks,
  Factory, Bunkry, Missile Turrety i Starport. Potem po jednym z pozostałych budynków (Academy,
  Armory, Comsat, Science Facility, Nuclear Silo...) i wszystkie 12 badań, jak AI StarCrafta.
- 12 planów lądowych, bierze pierwszy pasujący:
    - początek gry: rush piechotą, zwiad, obrona domu, dopóki nie ma Factory;
    - bio push: Marines i Firebaty po Academy (jak w StarCrafcie melee);
    - rush pojazdami, gdy stoi Factory;
    - rajdy Vulture'ów na twoich zbieraczy (gdy masz więcej niż jedną rafinerię);
    - duży rajd, gdy masz wiele rafinerii, atak z flanki na słabo bronione rafinerie;
    - trzymanie linii: straż domowa, gdy jesteś dwa razy silniejszy;
    - siege push: Siege Tanki z piechotą (jak AI StarCrafta w "The Trump Card");
    - zwykłe i szybkie ataki jako zapas. Ataki rosną z czasem.
- Flota powietrzna: Wraithy, Battlecruisery i Science Vessele ze Starportu atakują jako flota
  (nękanie i późne ataki floty, jak Battlecruisery AI StarCrafta).
- Siege Mode (łatka silnika): komputer rozkłada czołg dopiero, gdy cel jest w zasięgu (11,9 pola
  jako czołg, 20,4 w trybie oblężenia, minimum 3), i składa go z powrotem po ok. 2250 klatkach
  bez celu. Wcześniej rozkładał od razu, gdziekolwiek czołg stał, i nigdy go nie składał. AI
  Aliantów DTA też rozkłada teraz swojego Enforcera dopiero przy wrogu.
- Samoobrona: 34 bojowe jednostki Terran reagują w promieniu 12 pól (więcej przy broni o dłuższym
  zasięgu). Gdy zaatakujesz budynek komputera, przyjeżdżają jego wolne jednostki i drużyna, którą
  właśnie zbiera, i walczą. Atak na sam Bunkier albo działko nie wzywa pomocy. Twoich jednostek
  dotyczy to tylko w trybie Guard Area.
- Trudność: te same 7 poziomów AI co frakcje DTA.
- Nie zbuduje do walki Medyków, Dropshipów ani Valkyrii, nie robi ekspansji na mapach StarCrafta
  (AI szuka drzew Tyberium) i bez Advanced AI nie atakuje.
